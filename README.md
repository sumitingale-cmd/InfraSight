# INFRA SIGHT

## AI-Powered Early Warning & Decision Support for Land Acquisition Delays

**Smart India Hackathon 2026 | Problem ID: SIH26017**

> INFRA SIGHT is an AI-powered predictive analytics and decision-support system designed to identify potential delays in land acquisition projects at an early stage and support timely corrective action.

---

##  One-Line Project Description

**INFRA SIGHT monitors land acquisition projects, predicts delay risks, explains their causes, recommends corrective actions, and helps stakeholders track those actions.**

---

##  Problem Statement

Land acquisition is a critical process for infrastructure and development projects. However, projects can experience delays due to multiple factors such as:

- Delays in approvals and notifications
- Compensation and disbursement issues
- Legal disputes
- Ownership conflicts
- Documentation problems
- Rehabilitation and Resettlement (R&R) delays
- Delays in obtaining possession
- Stakeholder coordination issues
- Inter-departmental dependencies

Traditional monitoring approaches may identify problems only after significant delays have already occurred.

There is a need for a system that can analyze project information, identify early warning signals, predict potential delays, explain the reasons behind the risk, and support stakeholders in taking corrective action.

---

##  Proposed Solution

**INFRA SIGHT** provides an integrated predictive analytics and decision-support approach for land acquisition monitoring.

The system analyzes project-related information and provides:

- Project progress monitoring
- Delay risk prediction
- Risk-level identification
- Stage-wise delay analysis
- Explanation of major risk factors
- Supporting evidence for predictions
- Corrective recommendations
- Action assignment and tracking
- GIS-based visualization of project risks
- Project and regional analytics

The objective is to move from **reactive monitoring** to **early risk identification and proactive intervention**.

---

#  MONITOR → PREDICT → EXPLAIN → RECOMMEND → ACT

### 1.  MONITOR

Monitor the progress and current status of land acquisition projects.

The system can consider information such as:

- Project details
- Land area
- Affected families
- Compensation status
- Approval status
- Legal disputes
- Possession status
- Rehabilitation progress
- Stakeholder responsiveness
- Historical project performance

### 2. 🔮 PREDICT

Analyze available project information to identify projects that may be at risk of delay.

The system provides:

- Risk score
- Risk level
- Delay probability
- Stage at risk
- Project-level risk information

This helps stakeholders identify potential problems before they become critical.

### 3.  EXPLAIN

Instead of only displaying a risk score, INFRA SIGHT explains **why** a project has been identified as high risk.

The system highlights:

- Major contributing factors
- Risk indicators
- Relevant project information
- Supporting evidence

This improves transparency and helps stakeholders understand the prediction.

### 4.  RECOMMEND

Based on the identified risk factors, the system provides appropriate corrective recommendations.

Examples include:

- Follow-up on pending approvals
- Address compensation-related issues
- Review unresolved disputes
- Resolve documentation gaps
- Monitor rehabilitation progress
- Improve stakeholder coordination

### 5.  ACT

The final step converts recommendations into actionable tasks.

Stakeholders can:

- Assign actions
- Track action status
- Monitor progress
- Follow up on pending actions
- Review completed actions

This creates a complete cycle from **risk identification to corrective action**.

---

#  Key Features

-  **Project Monitoring** — Monitor land acquisition projects and their current progress.
-  **Risk Prediction** — Identify projects that may be vulnerable to delays.
-  **Explainable Risk Analysis** — Understand the factors contributing to the predicted risk.
-  **Evidence-Based Explanation** — Provide supporting project information behind identified risks.
-  **Corrective Recommendations** — Suggest actions based on identified risk factors.
-  **Action Tracking** — Assign and track corrective actions.
-  **GIS-Based Visualization** — Visualize project and geographical risk information.
-  **Analytics** — Analyze project-level and regional trends.
-  **Authentication** — Provide secure access to the platform.
