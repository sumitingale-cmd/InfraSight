# INFRA SIGHT

### AI-Powered Early Warning & Decision Support for Land Acquisition Delays

**Smart India Hackathon 2026 | Problem ID: SIH26017**

INFRA SIGHT is a predictive analytics and decision-support system designed to help identify land acquisition projects that are at risk of delay.

The system monitors project progress, predicts delay risks, explains the factors behind those predictions, recommends corrective actions, and helps stakeholders track the required actions.

---

##  Problem Statement

Land acquisition projects can face delays due to multiple factors such as:

- Approval and notification delays
- Legal disputes and ownership conflicts
- Compensation delays
- Documentation issues
- Rehabilitation and Resettlement (R&R) progress
- Stakeholder coordination issues
- Delays in taking possession of land

Identifying these risks at an early stage can help authorities take corrective action before delays become critical.

---

##  Our Solution

INFRA SIGHT follows a five-stage workflow:

**MONITOR → PREDICT → EXPLAIN → RECOMMEND → ACT**

### 1. MONITOR
Track the current status and progress of land acquisition projects.

### 2. PREDICT
Analyze project information and estimate the probability and level of delay risk.

### 3. EXPLAIN
Identify the major factors contributing to the predicted risk and provide supporting evidence from available project information.

### 4. RECOMMEND
Generate corrective recommendations based on the identified risk factors.

### 5. ACT
Assign, track, and monitor corrective actions to support timely intervention.
